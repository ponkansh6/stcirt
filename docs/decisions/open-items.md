# Open decisions and ownership gates

This register contains unresolved policy or operational questions. The observed code behavior in [`openspec/specs/stcirt/spec.md`](../../openspec/specs/stcirt/spec.md) remains a description of current behavior; it does not decide these questions. Product-owner, deployment/operator, and data-owner roles should be assigned through the project’s normal process. No individual is presumed responsible here.

| Decision                                                                                                        | Decision role                          | Decide before                                                 | If unanswered                                                                                     |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Intended use, concurrent participant capacity, acceptable latency, availability target                          | Product owner with deployment/operator | Committing to capacity, SLO, or load-dependent implementation | Keep capacity and SLO values unspecified; defer work that depends on them.                        |
| Whether a JEV retry scheduler is deployed; its cadence, monitoring, alerting, and operational owner             | Deployment/operator                    | Promising retry completion or operational coverage            | State only the repository behavior and that deployed scheduling is unverified.                    |
| Normal retention, correction/deletion process, backup retention and deletion propagation                        | Data owner with product owner          | Agreeing a retention period or deletion feature               | Do not invent retention periods or deletion guarantees.                                           |
| RPO, RTO, backup/restore authority, and response to accidental publication                                      | Deployment/operator with product owner | Promising recovery objectives or recovery service             | Keep RPO/RTO unresolved and do not claim recovery guarantees.                                     |
| Whether free-text answer content may be sent to the configured grading provider and any notice/consent controls | Product owner with data owner          | Expanding provider use or making a privacy promise            | Preserve the observed payload boundary and describe the PII risk; do not claim de-identification. |

Resolution procedure: record the decision, date, approving role, rationale, alternatives considered, and affected requirements in a focused document under `docs/decisions/`; update the relevant spec contract and operational guide in the same change. Until the decision is recorded, label it unresolved and keep any related promise or implementation gated.
